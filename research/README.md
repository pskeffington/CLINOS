# Literature and standards research

`literature_matrix.csv` is a **seed index**, not a completed validation audit. Each row has URL, scope, claims to verify, review status and limitations. Resolve project license, actual feature coverage, maintenance, compatibility, provenance and latest authoritative version before treating a row as evidence.

## Research tracks
- Standards: HL7 FHIR R4, US Core, SMART App Launch, USCDI, USCDI+ as applicable, HL7 v2, DICOM/DICOMweb, EHI export, X12 (license considerations).
- OSS: OpenEMR, OpenMRS, Medplum, HAPI FHIR, Inferno, Synthea, OHIF and Epic EHI research projects.
- Epic interoperability: public developer portal, EHI export schema and applicable legal/contract terms.
- Defect taxonomy: loss of units, terminology ambiguity, referential mismatch, identifier collision, amend/history loss, data provenance loss.
- Safety and regulatory scope: ONC certification program, privacy/security, clinical safety and independent validation.

## Inclusion protocol
Prefer specifications and primary project documentation. Record upstream license, commit/release version, retrieval date, tested interface, unsupported cases, and any claimed benchmark evidence. Distinguish source-described capability from independently reproduced capability. Log broken links and unavailable documents rather than fabricating findings.
